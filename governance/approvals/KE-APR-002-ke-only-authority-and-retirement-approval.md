4. accepts KE-ADR-001;
5. cancels the proposed relocation release and associated activation gates;
6. closes KE-EXC-001 as superseded;
7. removes the retired identity from the active platform portfolio;
8. authorizes removal of the migrated platform tree after absorption and verification;
9. authorizes replacement of product platform-adoption references with KE conformance records; and
10. permits archival or deletion of the external retired repository after zero-dependency verification.

## Durable Instruction

The controlling Product Owner instruction is that KE shall be maintained alone and no dependency shall remain on the retired platform.
