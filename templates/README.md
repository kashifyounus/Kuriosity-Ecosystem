# KE Governed Template Catalog

These templates operationalize existing KE authority. They are non-normative aids and do not create approval, conformance, admission, or release by themselves.

| Template | Controlled by | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| [Platform mandate](ke-platform-mandate-template.md) | KE-002, KE-004, KE-007 | Prepare a recognized platform mandate |
| [Platform admission assessment](ke-platform-admission-assessment-template.md) | KE-002, KE-004, KE-REV-001 | Evaluate admission evidence |
| [Product conformance](ke-product-conformance-template.md) | KE-002, KE-007 | Record a product's declared KE adoption |
| [Deviation](ke-deviation-template.md) | KE-007 | Bound and approve an exception |
| [Engineering review](ke-engineering-review-template.md) | KE-REV-001 | Record risk-scaled review evidence |
| [Architecture decision](ke-architecture-decision-template.md) | KE-ARCH-001 | Preserve an architecture decision and consequences |
| [Release evidence](ke-release-evidence-template.md) | KE-RLS-001, KE-007 | Assemble release evidence without implying approval |
| [Assurance profile](ke-assurance-profile-template.md) | KE-SEC-001, KE-DATA-001, KE-AI-001 | Record applicable security, data, and AI controls |

Replace every bracketed instruction. Remove inapplicable sections only with a recorded rationale. A completed template remains a proposal until its competent human authority approves it through the applicable KE process.
