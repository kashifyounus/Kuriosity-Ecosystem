# KE-COM-001 — Engineering Communication Standard

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.0 |
| Status | Effective |
| Authority | KE-005 and KE-REV-001 |
| Owner | Ecosystem Engineering Authority |
| Effective Date | 2026-07-29 |

## 1. Principles

Material communication shall be truthful, direct, precise, evidence-based, decision-focused, risk-aware, and proportionate.

It shall distinguish verified fact, source-derived statement, analysis, inference, assumption, recommendation, decision, approval, risk, uncertainty, blocked work, deferred work, and verified result.

## 2. Evidence

Claims of completion, correctness, security, compliance, compatibility, readiness, performance, or approval shall cite appropriate evidence or authority. Confidence, fluency, seniority, or length is not evidence.

## 3. Structure

Communication shall lead with outcome, decision, risk, required action, or verified status. It may use Facts, Assumptions, Analysis, Recommendation, Risks, Decision, Verification, Blockers, Deferred Work, and Required Action when those labels improve clarity.

## 4. Recommendation

A material recommendation identifies the option, problem, constraints, rationale, tradeoffs, risks, dependencies, required authority, and reconsideration trigger.

## 5. Required Action

Communication shall clearly separate Product Owner action, engineering action, reviewer action, other authority action, completed work, blocked work, and information requiring no action.

## 6. Final for Scope

Final communication shall address the approved scope, disclose limitations and uncertainty, identify unresolved decisions, state verification actually performed, and avoid adding unrelated recommendations after closure.

## 7. AI Communication

AI shall not claim it read, executed, inspected, verified, approved, or completed something it did not. Private reasoning need not be disclosed, but material sources, assumptions, limitations, and evidence shall be.
