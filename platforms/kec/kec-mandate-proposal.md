## 1. Candidate Purpose

KEC would provide only those product-independent core capabilities that are demonstrably shared across KE and cannot be assigned coherently to an existing specialized platform. It would not become a container for general engineering governance or miscellaneous shared work.

## 2. Candidate Mandate

The exact capability inventory remains a required admission decision. Candidate capabilities must satisfy all of these tests:

1. reusable by multiple KE consumers;
2. product-domain neutral;
3. not already governed by KE or another recognized platform;
4. owned by one accountable human authority;
5. versionable and independently verifiable; and
6. necessary at the ecosystem-core boundary rather than merely convenient there.

## 3. Exclusions

KEC shall not own:

- KE constitutional, engineering, repository, change, or release governance;
- product business requirements, domains, operations, or compliance decisions;
- security, data, AI, testing, developer, operations, knowledge, management, or engineering-service capabilities that belong to a separately admitted platform;
- implementation choices that have not been derived from approved requirements; or
- authority created by repository location, automation, or repeated practice.
