# KE-HIST-001 — Retired Engineering-Platform Evidence Record

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Classification | Historical; non-normative |
| Status | Final |

## Purpose

This self-contained record preserves sufficient evidence that a former engineering-platform governance baseline existed, was migrated temporarily, and was then retired. It creates no current authority and requires no external repository.

## Preserved Facts

- A Version 1.0.0 governance baseline was ratified in July 2026.
- Its principal instruments addressed founding purpose, platform scope, founding decisions, engineering obligations, communication, artifact naming, Product Owner interaction, and engineering review.
- A Version 1.0.1 relocation candidate was prepared but never became effective.
- KE absorbed the useful product-independent obligations through KE-005, KE-006, KE-007, KE-GOV-001, KE-PO-001, KE-REV-001, and KE-COM-001.
- KE-ADR-001 retired the separate platform identity and KE-APR-002 approved removal of its active repository content and dependencies.

## Authority Effect

Former documents, identifiers, releases, coordinates, and repositories are superseded and non-normative. They shall not be used for current adoption, conformance, interpretation, rollback, release, or maintenance.

Git history and merged pull requests preserve additional audit evidence without making that history an active dependency.
