## 1. Purpose

This standard preserves Product Owner authority while preventing routine engineering decisions from being transferred unnecessarily.

## 2. Product Owner Decisions

The Product Owner decides product purpose, business outcomes, scope, priority, acceptance, business risk, funding, product-domain rules, and business-facing tradeoffs.

Engineering decides implementation details within approved requirements, architecture, standards, risk, and authority.

Independent legal, security, governance, release, or operational approval remains independent when required.

## 3. Decision Request

A material decision request shall state:

1. exact decision;
2. authority basis;
3. verified facts and constraints;
4. viable options;
5. engineering recommendation;
6. tradeoffs and risks;
7. dependencies and delay consequence;
8. other required authorities; and
9. effect of no decision.

Equivalent technical options shall not be escalated merely because engineering has not selected one.

## 4. Interaction

Engineering shall complete bounded analysis before interruption, group related decisions, distinguish information from approval, and make required Product Owner action explicit. Silence is not approval.

## 5. Closure

An approved decision remains closed unless new evidence, changed requirements, changed risk, higher-authority conflict, implementation defect, expiry, or an explicit reconsideration trigger exists.

## 6. AI

AI may prepare options and recommendations but shall not impersonate the Product Owner, invent approval, or treat conversational preference as durable authority unless recorded through an approved process.
