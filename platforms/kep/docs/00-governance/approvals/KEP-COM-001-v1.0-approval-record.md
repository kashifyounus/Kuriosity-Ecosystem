## Approval Statement

KEP-COM-001 Version 1.0 is approved as the binding subordinate Engineering Communication Standard. It establishes communication classifications, status controls, final-answer readiness, recommendation and decision closure, required-action clarity, information-density rules, self-reference restrictions, draft handling, risk communication, and AI-assisted presentation requirements within its stated authority and scope.

No constitutional conflict or blocking governance dependency was identified at approval. UD-010 remains unresolved and continues to limit platform-wide retention requirements for intermediate AI execution records; it does not prevent this standard from becoming effective.
