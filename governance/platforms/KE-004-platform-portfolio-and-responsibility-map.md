## 3. Engineering Governance Boundary

Reusable engineering governance is owned directly by KE through KE-005, KE-006, KE-007, approved standards, methodologies, patterns, templates, and decisions. It is not delegated to a separate platform.

## 4. Admission Requirements

Before a recognized platform becomes admitted, its governance shall define identity, purpose, mandate, exclusions, owner, architecture and authority boundaries, dependencies, product relationship, release model, verification, and effective date.

## 5. Boundary Rules

- A directory or name does not establish a mandate.
- A platform owns only reusable capabilities within its ratified boundary.
- Products retain business-domain authority.
- Cross-platform capabilities have one accountable owner.
- A placeholder platform is not released or adoptable.
- Retired identities are not active portfolio members.
