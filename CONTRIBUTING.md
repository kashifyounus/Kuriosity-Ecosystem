# Contributing to Kuriosity Ecosystem

KE is a governed engineering repository, not an unbounded content collection. Contributions must preserve the authority hierarchy and the ecosystem/platform/product boundary.

## Contribution Rules

1. Open a focused branch and pull request; do not publish directly to `main`.
2. Identify the governing authority, scope, change class, consumers, compatibility effect, evidence, owner, and required approver.
3. Extend an existing concept before proposing a new one.
4. Keep product business knowledge in the product repository. KE content must remain reusable and product-independent, except for explicit conformance or evidence records.
5. Update affected links, registers, manifests, and supersession records.
6. Run `python tools/validate_repository.py` and include the result.
7. Do not commit secrets, personal data, credentials, or restricted records.

A merged pull request does not by itself ratify an artifact. Only the competent human authority and applicable KE process may approve, admit, release, or supersede authority.
