## 1. Purpose

This Charter establishes the founding identity, mission, intended outcomes, initial scope, and formation boundaries of the Kuriosity Ecosystem.

It is subordinate to KE-000 and does not become governing authority until ratified.

## 2. Founding Mission

KE exists to provide a coherent ecosystem in which reusable platforms can be governed, evolved, adopted, and verified without absorbing product-domain authority or duplicating product knowledge.

## 3. Founding Outcomes

KE is established to enable:

- clear separation between ecosystem, platform, and product authority;
- reusable engineering and operational capabilities with accountable ownership;
- explicit, versioned adoption of platform capabilities by products;
- durable and discoverable governing knowledge;
- deterministic verification of authority, conformance, and release claims;
- controlled ecosystem evolution without silent transfer of responsibility; and
- the minimum sufficient governance required to preserve coherence and trust.

## 4. Ecosystem Scope

KE governs shared ecosystem concerns, including:

- ecosystem identity and constitutional authority;
- admission, classification, lifecycle, and retirement of KE platforms;
- cross-platform authority boundaries and dependency rules;
- ecosystem-wide governance, standards, and architectural decisions;
- the rules by which products adopt and deviate from KE platforms;
- repository-controlled preservation of KE authority and evidence; and
- ecosystem-level verification and release declarations.

KE does not govern a product's business purpose, business requirements, domain model, product data, product operations, or product-specific compliance decisions.

## 5. Initial Platform Portfolio

The founding portfolio recognizes the following platform identities:

- KEC — Kuriosity Ecosystem Core;
- KEM — Kuriosity Ecosystem Management;
- KKP — Kuriosity Knowledge Platform;
- KEP — Kuriosity Engineering Platform;
- KES — Kuriosity Engineering Services;
- KDS — Kuriosity Developer Services;
- KTP — Kuriosity Testing Platform;
- KSP — Kuriosity Security Platform;
- KDP — Kuriosity Data Platform;
- KOP — Kuriosity Operations Platform; and
- KAS — Kuriosity AI Services.

Recognition of a platform identity does not, by itself, approve a mandate, capability, dependency, release, or product adoption obligation. Those matters require ratified platform boundaries and appropriate subordinate governance.

## 6. Founding Principles

KE shall:

- preserve the constitutional authority and boundaries established by KE-000;
- extend existing concepts before creating new ones;
- keep platform knowledge product-independent;
- require explicit human accountability for governing decisions;
- preserve uncertainty and unresolved decisions rather than invent authority;
- prefer deterministic and reviewable evidence;
- keep approved artifacts versioned, discoverable, and traceable; and
- evolve through controlled change with backward compatibility considered before breaking change.

## 7. Repository as Durable Record

The canonical KE repository shall preserve approved specifications, decisions, registers, evidence, and release records.

Repository presence alone does not make an artifact authoritative. Each normative artifact shall declare its identifier, version, lifecycle state, owner, authority level, and effective status.

Conversation history and automated output may support drafting and review but shall not replace repository-controlled approval.

## 8. Founding-Stage Controls

During the founding stage:

- one person may hold multiple accountable roles when explicitly recorded;
- delegation of execution does not transfer accountability;
- proposed platform mandates remain non-authoritative until ratified;
- unresolved licensing, distribution, validation, or release conditions shall remain visible;
- historical release records shall not be silently rewritten; and
- publication to the authoritative branch requires verification evidence.

## 9. Ratification Conditions

This Charter may be ratified only after:

1. consistency with KE-000 is verified;
2. the platform identities in Section 5 are confirmed;
3. the relationship to the KE Operating Model and Authority Model is reviewed;
4. unresolved founding decisions are explicitly recorded; and
5. the Founding Authority records approval and an effective date.
