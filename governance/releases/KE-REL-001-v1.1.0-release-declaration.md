# KE-REL-001 — KE v1.1.0 Release Declaration

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Release | KE v1.1.0 |
| Status | Effective |
| Release Date | 2026-07-29 |
| Authority | Kuriosity Ecosystem Founding Authority |

KE v1.1.0 establishes KE as the sole maintained engineering-governance authority.

This release:

- supersedes the KE v1.0 foundation where instruments were revised;
- internalizes reusable engineering governance;
- removes the retired engineering platform from the active portfolio;
- cancels its relocation candidate and visibility exception;
- removes active external repository coordinates;
- introduces KE-native repository, change, release, conformance, deviation, review, communication, naming, and Product Owner governance; and
- authorizes deletion or archival of the retired external repository after verified publication.

Compatibility: product business authority is unchanged. Products shall replace former platform-adoption references with KE v1.1.0 conformance records during their next controlled repository update.
