# Kuriosity Ecosystem (KE)

The Kuriosity Ecosystem is the governed parent ecosystem for reusable platforms and the products that adopt them.

## Authority

Repository-controlled, approved artifacts are the durable source of truth for KE. Conversation history, automation output, implementation precedent, and repository proximity do not create governing authority.

The current authority order begins with:

1. applicable law and binding legal obligations;
2. binding contracts;
3. [KE-000 — Kuriosity Ecosystem Constitution](governance/constitution/KE-000-kuriosity-ecosystem-constitution.md);
4. ratified subordinate KE governance; and
5. approved platform and product governance within their declared boundaries.

## Repository Map

- `governance/` — KE constitutional and subordinate governance.
- `platforms/` — independently governed reusable platforms.
- `standards/` — ecosystem-wide standards approved under KE authority.
- `methodologies/` — ecosystem-wide methods approved for reusable use.
- `patterns/` — reusable, non-mandatory solution patterns.
- `adr/` — KE-level architecture decisions.
- `templates/` — governed reusable artifact templates.
- `reference/` — catalogs, registers, terminology, and non-normative reference material.
- `docs/` — plans, reports, verification, migration evidence, and repository guidance.

## Platform and Product Boundary

Platforms govern reusable capabilities within ratified mandates. Products govern their business domains. Products adopt platforms through explicit, versioned composition and do not redefine platform or ecosystem authority.

## Publication Rule

Only artifacts marked Ratified, Approved, Effective, or Published by the competent authority are normative. Draft, proposed, planned, reserved, and verification artifacts are not normative unless an approved governing artifact expressly incorporates them.
