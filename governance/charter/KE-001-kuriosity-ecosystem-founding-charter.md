## 1. Purpose

This Charter establishes KE as the single governed ecosystem for reusable engineering governance, reusable capability platforms, and product conformance.

## 2. Mission

KE enables products to be conceived, engineered, reviewed, implemented, verified, released, operated, and evolved through explicit authority, durable knowledge, reusable standards, and evidence.

## 3. Founding Outcomes

KE shall provide:

- clear ecosystem, platform, and product authority boundaries;
- direct ecosystem ownership of reusable engineering governance;
- reusable platforms with accountable and bounded mandates;
- explicit product conformance, adoption, extension, and deviation records;
- deterministic verification of authority, quality, completion, and release claims;
- responsible human-governed use of automation and artificial intelligence; and
- controlled evolution without silent transfer of ownership or authority.

## 4. Ecosystem Scope

KE governs:

- ecosystem identity and constitutional authority;
- engineering principles, lifecycle obligations, quality gates, and review standards;
- repository, documentation, knowledge, and release governance;
- platform admission, classification, dependency, and retirement;
- product conformance, platform adoption, extension, and deviation;
- cross-platform architecture and shared standards; and
- ecosystem-level verification and release declarations.

Products retain authority over their business purpose, requirements, domains, data, operations, and product-specific compliance decisions.

## 5. Platform Portfolio

The founding portfolio recognizes:

- KEC — Kuriosity Ecosystem Core;
- KEM — Kuriosity Ecosystem Management;
- KKP — Kuriosity Knowledge Platform;
- KES — Kuriosity Engineering Services;
- KDS — Kuriosity Developer Services;
- KTP — Kuriosity Testing Platform;
- KSP — Kuriosity Security Platform;
- KDP — Kuriosity Data Platform;
- KOP — Kuriosity Operations Platform; and
- KAS — Kuriosity AI Services.

Recognition does not approve a mandate, release, dependency, or product adoption obligation.

## 6. Founding Principles

KE shall:

- apply engineering before implementation and governance before automation;
- preserve requirements, decisions, evidence, and accountability;
- extend existing concepts before creating new ones;
- keep ecosystem and platform knowledge product-independent;
- prefer deterministic, explainable, traceable, auditable, modular, reusable, and maintainable outcomes;
- keep approved artifacts versioned, discoverable, and internally linked;
- preserve backward compatibility when practical; and
- record exceptions, unresolved decisions, and breaking changes explicitly.

## 7. Repository Authority

`kashifyounus/Kuriosity-Ecosystem` is the sole canonical repository for KE authority. No external repository is required to interpret, adopt, verify, release, or maintain KE.

Repository presence alone does not make an artifact authoritative. Every normative artifact shall declare its identifier, version, status, owner, governing authority, and effective date.
