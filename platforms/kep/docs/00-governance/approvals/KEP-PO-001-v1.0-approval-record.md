## Approval Statement

KEP-PO-001 Version 1.0 is approved as the binding subordinate Product Owner Interaction Standard. It establishes Product Owner decision boundaries, engineering self-resolution duties, decision routing, interruption controls, acceptance boundaries, closure rules, and AI-assisted interaction requirements within its stated authority and scope.

No constitutional conflict or blocking governance dependency was identified at approval. UD-014 remains unresolved and continues to limit formal conflict-of-interest and appeal mechanisms; it does not prevent this standard from becoming effective.
