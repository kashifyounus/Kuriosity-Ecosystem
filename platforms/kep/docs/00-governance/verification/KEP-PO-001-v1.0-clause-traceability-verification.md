## 1. Determination

KEP-PO-001 Version 1.0 is traceable to existing KEP authority and introduces only subordinate operational controls within the approved scope. No clause changes constitutional precedence, human accountability, R0–R4 risk classes, amendment authority, or deferred-decision status.

The standard correctly separates Product Owner authority, engineering authority, governance authority, and cross-authority approval. It does not assign accountable authority to AI and does not treat Product Owner approval as a substitute for independently required architecture, security, quality, legal, release, or governance approval.

## 4. Duplication Review

The standard references and operationalizes higher-authority duties without attempting to replace their constitutional meaning. Limited restatement is necessary to make decision routing executable. No prohibited constitutional duplication was identified.

## 5. Deferred-Decision Review
